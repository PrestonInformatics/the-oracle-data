Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Preston Informatics respects your privacy. The Oracle Engine operates on a strict offline-first architecture. All algorithmic prediction calculations, ticket history logs, and visual analytics execute locally on your mobile device CPU.

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or monitor any personal user data, location data, financial records, or device identifiers.
  • Local SQLite Storage: All generated prediction slates and pick history records remain stored exclusively in your device's local encrypted storage.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not integrate third-party advertising, analytics tracking SDKs, or data broker networks.
